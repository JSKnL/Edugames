SLS Learning Objectives (Primary 3 Math)

  • Denominator Range: Covers halves, thirds, quarters, fifths, sixths, eighths, tenths, and twelfths ($\frac{a}{2}$ to $\frac{a}{12}$).
  • Level 1: Express 1 Whole as fractions with different denominators ($\frac{2}{2}, \frac{4}{4}, \frac{8}{8}, \frac{12}{12} = 1$).
  • Level 2: Add and Subtract like fractions within 1 Whole ($\frac{a}{d} \pm \frac{b}{d}$).

💡 Key Math Learning Rule

When the Numerator (top number) equals the Denominator (bottom number), the fraction equals 1 Whole! (e.g. $\frac{2}{2} = \frac{6}{6} = \frac{12}{12} = 1$). When adding or subtracting like fractions, keep the denominator the same!
