🎨 What is Notan?

Notan (濃淡) is a Japanese art idea meaning dark–light. In a notan design, dark and light shapes are balanced, and they swap places in the mirror image.

Example: the right side is the mirror image of the left side, but every dark square becomes light and every light square becomes dark.

How to play

  1. Look at the pattern in the given half. Both dark and light squares matter.
  2. Find the red dashed mirror line. It can be vertical, horizontal or diagonal.
  3. For each square, find its mirror partner on the other side of the line.
  4. Give the partner the opposite colour. Click the partner of every light square to make it dark.
  5. Press Check. Stuck? Use 💡 Hint. Or press 👀 Show answer (no point for that puzzle).
  6. In the last puzzle you design your own notan!

Scoring: 1 point for each correct answer. Total points are shown at the top.
